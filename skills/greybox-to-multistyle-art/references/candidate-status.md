# Current scope and evidence boundary

The package's exact version and bytes are identified by `greybox.py --version` and `MANIFEST.json`. Use this page to choose an available route and identify what still needs checking in the actual host, project and target device.

## Asset route checkpoint

The current package requires explicit form versus surface/light/effect ownership and a generated/reuse/procedural evaluation for dominant form sources before final art production. AI/hybrid sources retain their actual image-conditioned operations and original completed model-task identity before H4; pending model work cannot silently become code geometry. Native-only effects, suitable reuse and visually proven rule-expressible forms remain valid. These are local evidence/entrypoint checks, not a global sandbox around arbitrary host tools. Historical v2 sample sources retain their original policy and are explicitly reported as historical rather than relabeled as v3-verified.

## Main capability

One gameplay greybox becomes playable prototypes in multiple art styles. The runtime defaults to multistyle with at least two styles; an explicitly scoped single-style mode remains available. Shared baseline/resources, per-style ownership, H1–H5, a complete priority sample before expansion, cumulative repairs and original supplier ledgers govern the work. The final playable delivery covers every selected style and promised region.

Batching follows actual dependencies and account limits. Native tools are agent-driven unless their real host demonstrates independent dispatch. A configured adapter, successful setup or completed asset is not proof of useful art, whole-scene playability or human acceptance.

## Limits that affect a real task

- **Visual quality:** actual returned images, crops, GLBs and whole-scene native views need inspection. Hashes, schemas and declared reviews cannot understand pixels or authenticate the reviewer. Required failed/unclear conditions remain unresolved.
- **Performance:** processing targets are budgets, not device measurements. Remesh/rig/motion is conditional and operation-specific; inspect textures, silhouette, scale and necessary deformation/contact after changes. No automatic universal performance guarantee is made.
- **Production and cost:** configure only the chosen operation. Accounts, currencies, quotes, reserves and actual charges remain distinct. Unknown submissions recover before retries; local account capacity does not create a case-wide spending authorization.
- **Compatibility:** current-version Windows behavior, real host sessions, provider useful output, full colleague workflows, broad efficiency and human acceptance need their own evidence. Reuse valid historical observations without relabeling their version; revalidate only affected runtime-bound results.
- **Storage and privacy:** the package excludes game assets, credentials, original approvals and private recovery URLs. Keep canonical hashed resources and small scoped evidence; avoid full-project snapshots.

[Workflow risks](workflow-risks.md) maps these limits to next actions.
