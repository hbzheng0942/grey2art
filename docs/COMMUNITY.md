## Questions and contributions

Use this repository's Issues for reproducible bugs and feature proposals. Include the Skill version, host and engine, expected result, and a small reproduction. [Support](SUPPORT.md)

To share a prototype, include the greybox, actual art variants, a short gameplay recording and a source or playable link. [Contribute a showcase](../CONTRIBUTING.md#showcase-contributions)

Keep sensitive reports private. [Security reporting](../SECURITY.md)
